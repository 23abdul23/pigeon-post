Delete your Pigeon Post account

Last updated: 2026-11-02. Draft pending owner and legal review.

You can delete your Pigeon Post account and its data in either of two ways.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Pigeon Post and go to Settings.
  2. Open the Account section and choose Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and the data stored for it on our servers are deleted when you confirm, and the data stored on your device is cleared.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app, email privacy@pigeonpost.app from the address you used to sign in, and we will delete your account. If you cannot sign in, contact us at that address and we will help.

What is deleted

Your profile (email, name, picture), home city, journeys, stories, stamps, letters and friend connections stored in our AWS (us-east-1) database and storage. Reports about abuse may be kept for up to 12 months for moderation, then removed. Deletion cannot be undone.

See the Privacy Policy for more.
