Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02. Draft pending owner and legal review.

Pigeon Post (app id app.pigeonpost) lets you send virtual pigeons on journeys between cities and exchange letters with friends. This policy explains what we collect and why. Contact: privacy@pigeonpost.app.

Who can use Pigeon Post

You must be 13 or older, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

What we collect

  • Account details from Google sign-in (through Amazon Cognito): your email address, name and profile picture.
  • Your home city, which you pick yourself. The app does not ask for location permission and never collects your device location.
  • Content you create: journeys, stories, stamps, letters and friend connections.
  • A copy of your data stored on your device so the app works offline.
  • On Android, a push notification token: an identifier issued by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for this app on this device. We use it only to send you an "A letter arrived" notification when you have letter alerts on, and it contains only the sender's first name, never the letter. We keep it until you sign out, delete your account, or the token stops being valid.
  • Reports and blocks you make against other users, kept for moderation.

The app shows no ads, offers no purchases and currently includes no analytics SDK.

How we use it

To sign you in, run the app, deliver letters to friends, show your passport and journal, send notifications you ask for, and review reports of abuse. Friends see only your city, never exact coordinates, and only what your privacy settings allow.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Amazon Web Services (DynamoDB and S3) in the us-east-1 region (United States). If you are outside the United States, your data is transferred there.

Third-party services

The app loads map tiles, place, weather, bird-observation and encyclopedia data from services such as OpenFreeMap, GeoNames, Open-Meteo, GBIF, iNaturalist and Wikipedia. Those requests may expose your IP address to them. We do not send them your account details. See the Credits screen in the app for sources.

Retention

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Reports are kept for up to 12 months.

Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA you may ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, or export of your data. Email privacy@pigeonpost.app. You can delete your account yourself in Settings; see how to delete your account.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
