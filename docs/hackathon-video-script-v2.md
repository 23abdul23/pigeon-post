## 2. Numbers (0:20 – 0:40)

SAY (about 40 words): "The cost is real. We switch screens every forty-seven seconds. We are pinged every two minutes. And one interruption can take over twenty minutes to recover from."

SHOW: three number cards, one per beat, each with its source line under it in small type. A fourth beat shows the placeholder card until real data is found, then it is cut or filled.

Statistics (one per line, each with its source):

- **47 seconds**: average time attention stays on any one screen. Source: Gloria Mark, UC Irvine, *Attention Span* (2023), https://gloriamark.com/attention-span/
- **Every 2 minutes (275 times a day)**: average interruption by a meeting, email or notification for Microsoft 365 users during core work hours. Source: Microsoft Work Trend Index, "Breaking down the infinite workday" (2025), https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/breaking-down-infinite-workday
- **23 minutes 15 seconds**: average time to get back to a task after an interruption. Source: Gloria Mark (UC Irvine), as cited by Lifehacker and Addy Osmani; related primary paper: Mark, Gudith and Klocke, "The Cost of Interrupted Work: More Speed and Stress", CHI 2008, https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf. **Check before publishing:** I found the 23:15 figure only in secondary sources, not in the CHI 2008 paper itself.
- **[PLACEHOLDER: need data]**: share of students who study with a phone within reach or more than N browser tabs open.
- **[PLACEHOLDER: need data]**: number of people who use focus timers and the share who quit within a week. Do not use a user count for Pigeon Post unless you have real analytics.

Our own numbers (from the app, safe to show):

- **1 focus minute = 100 km.** Source: the app (`KM_PER_FOCUS_MINUTE`), shown on the Flight Desk, frame 02.
- **Zagreb to Kyoto is 9,190 km, about four 25-minute sessions.** Source: the app's route readout in frame 02 (9,190 km), divided by 2,500 km per 25-minute session (3.7, rounded up to four).

- Frames 11 and 12 were captured by closing and reopening the flight page, because the browser here never reports a tab as hidden. The real tab-switch (the proof moment) still has to be recorded live. The prompt text is "Let's continue your journey", not "Welcome Back".
- Home-city mismatch: frame 03 (focus map) was captured with home set to Samobor; every other frame uses Zagreb. Retake 03 if you want an exact match.
- Header changes in the app: the handwritten sub-labels and heading accents were removed from every screen header, and the top-bar title no longer wraps (see frames 02, 09, 10). Frame 05 is a single 780x5520 tall image of the whole Passport screen, with the bottom nav hidden for the capture.
